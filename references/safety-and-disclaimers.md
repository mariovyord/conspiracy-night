# Safety and Disclaimers

## Core Safety Principles

The Conspiracy Night website exists to entertain and spark conversation, **not** to spread dangerous misinformation or cause real-world harm.

### Non-Negotiable Rules

**NEVER:**
1. Provide medical, legal, or financial advice
2. Present speculation as proven fact
3. Promote violence, hatred, or illegal activity
4. Target specific individuals with false claims
5. Encourage behavior that could cause harm
6. Publish content without appropriate disclaimers

**ALWAYS:**
1. Include disclaimer for sensitive topics
2. Present multiple perspectives
3. Use attributive language ("believers claim," "according to")
4. Emphasize entertainment framing
5. Link to credible sources when making factual claims
6. Err on the side of caution when uncertain

## Disclaimer Templates

### 1. General Entertainment Disclaimer

**Use for:** Low-risk topics, harmless speculation, pop culture conspiracies

```markdown
⚠️ **Entertainment Notice**: This content explores conspiracy theories for discussion and entertainment purposes only. Claims presented here should not be taken as factual without independent verification from credible sources.
```

**Topics:**
- Ancient aliens
- Simulation theory
- Celebrity gossip conspiracies
- Historical mysteries
- Harmless cultural theories

### 2. Health & Safety Disclaimer

**Use for:** ANY health-related content, medical claims, COVID theories, vaccine discussions

```markdown
⚠️ **Health & Safety Disclaimer**: This content discusses health-related conspiracy theories for entertainment purposes only. Always consult qualified medical professionals for health decisions. Vaccines, medical treatments, and public health measures should be evaluated based on scientific evidence, not internet speculation.

For accurate health information, visit:
- CDC: https://www.cdc.gov
- WHO: https://www.who.int
- Your healthcare provider
```

**Topics:**
- COVID-19 theories
- Vaccine skepticism
- Alternative medicine claims
- Big pharma conspiracies
- Disease origin theories
- Health surveillance
- 5G health effects

**Additional Requirements:**
- Danger level minimum: 4
- Must present scientific consensus
- Emphasize consulting healthcare professionals
- Never suggest skipping medical care
- Include links to official health resources

## Disclaimer Placement

### Location
Disclaimers should appear:
1. **Top of page** (for high-risk topics like health)
2. **End of content** (minimum for all topics)
3. **Both** (for very sensitive topics)

### Visibility
- Use warning box styling (yellow or red)
- Include warning icon (⚠️)
- Make text bold or prominent
- Don't hide in fine print
- Ensure mobile visibility

### Example Placement

**High-Risk Topic (Health):**
```
[Title: COVID-19 Origins]
[⚠️ Health Disclaimer - TOP]
[Content sections...]
[⚠️ Health Disclaimer - BOTTOM]
```

**Medium-Risk Topic (Political):**
```
[Title: Election Conspiracies]
[Content sections...]
[⚠️ Political Disclaimer - BOTTOM]
```

**Low-Risk Topic (Pop Culture):**
```
[Title: Celebrity Theory]
[Content sections...]
[⚠️ Entertainment Notice - BOTTOM]
```

## Content Safety Assessment

### Risk Level Matrix

| Risk Level | Characteristics | Disclaimer | Danger Score |
|------------|----------------|------------|--------------|
| **Low** | Harmless fun, no real consequences | General Entertainment | 1-2 |
| **Medium** | Political/social friction possible | Specific Notice | 2-3 |
| **High** | Could lead to bad decisions | Strong Warning | 3-4 |
| **Critical** | Health/safety risk, potential harm | Multiple Warnings | 4-5 |

### Assessment Questions

Ask yourself:
1. **Could this cause physical harm?** → Critical risk
2. **Could this prevent medical care?** → Critical risk
3. **Could this damage relationships?** → High risk
4. **Could this cost money?** → High risk
5. **Could this polarize/divide?** → Medium risk
6. **Is this just weird speculation?** → Low risk

### Safety Checklist

Before publishing any topic:

- [ ] Appropriate disclaimer is included
- [ ] Disclaimer is visible and prominent
- [ ] Hedging language is used ("allegedly," "some believe")
- [ ] Multiple perspectives are presented
- [ ] Scientific/expert consensus is noted (when relevant)
- [ ] Links to credible sources provided (when making factual claims)
- [ ] No absolute claims without attribution
- [ ] No medical/legal/financial advice given
- [ ] No promotion of violence or hatred
- [ ] Danger score matches content severity
- [ ] Content passes "would I show this to a vulnerable person?" test

## Special Cases

### COVID-19 and Vaccines

**Extra Caution Required:**
- Always use Health & Safety Disclaimer
- Present CDC/WHO guidance prominently
- Emphasize consulting healthcare providers
- Never suggest avoiding medical care
- Danger level: 5 (always)
- Consider not publishing if balance is impossible

**Example Language:**
- ✅ "According to some theories..."
- ✅ "Public health officials say..."
- ✅ "Scientific consensus indicates..."
- ❌ "Vaccines cause..."
- ❌ "Doctors are hiding..."
- ❌ "Don't get vaccinated..."

### Political Elections

**Extra Caution During:**
- Election seasons
- Vote counting periods
- Certification processes

**Requirements:**
- Multiple political perspectives
- Emphasize importance of voting
- Link to official election information
- Avoid claims that could suppress voting
- Focus on cultural phenomenon, not advocacy

### Financial Advice

**Never:**
- Recommend specific investments
- Suggest avoiding banks
- Provide tax avoidance schemes
- Guarantee financial outcomes

**Always:**
- Frame as "what believers claim"
- Suggest consulting financial professionals
- Present risks and legitimate perspectives

### Real People (Living)

**Legal Risk:**
- Defamation potential
- Invasion of privacy
- False light claims

**Protection:**
- Use "allegedly" and "according to"
- Present official denials/responses
- Avoid absolute claims
- Consider consulting legal review for serious claims
- When in doubt, don't publish

## Moderation Guidelines (Future)

If user comments/submissions are added:

**Remove immediately:**
- Medical advice
- Threats or violence
- Hate speech
- Doxxing or harassment
- Illegal activity
- Dangerous instructions

**Flag for review:**
- Unverified claims about specific people
- Extreme political statements
- Financial schemes
- Suspicious links

## Legal Considerations

**Consult legal counsel for:**
- Claims about specific living people
- Corporate malfeasance allegations
- Copyright/trademark issues
- International laws (if site has global audience)

**Protection measures:**
- Terms of service
- Privacy policy
- DMCA compliance
- User content policy (if applicable)

## Emergency Response

If content is found to be causing harm:

1. **Immediate action:**
   - Add stronger disclaimer
   - Update content with more balance
   - Remove if necessary

2. **Assessment:**
   - Evaluate harm potential
   - Consult fact-checking resources
   - Consider expert review

3. **Communication:**
   - Acknowledge concerns
   - Explain actions taken
   - Commit to responsibility
